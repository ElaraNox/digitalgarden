### Journal of [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Seraphina\|Seraphina]]

We headed back to town after learning all we could from the Proclaimers, which was not much.

Upon arrival, we went directly to the Obelisk to find Alaric. He had rune rubbings and notes referencing a tiger, a scorpion, and an elephant. Aurelia held a black scorpion statuette.

---

We approached the Obelisk and noticed an opening leading inside. We decided to enter.

We descended stairs and found a pool of thick, viscous liquid that felt warm and comforting. In the center was a dais with a forcefield around it.

---

Inside the room, the doors and ceiling closed. The room went completely dark—no light, no sound, even my sight granted by the Raven Queen was absent.

The liquid began to move, crawling up the walls and onto the ceiling. As it did, the walls showed tiny silver pinpricks like eyes watching us.

We all climbed onto the dais. As we did, a voice spoke: “Welcome, do you seek knowledge?”

Gabriel: “If by knowledge, you mean answers.”

Myself: “If by knowledge, you mean knowledge, yes.” All of the eyes turned toward me.

From the pool, shadowy versions of us emerged, each carrying dual scimitars and attacking their real counterparts.

Zamira was knocked unconscious by her clone, which then disappeared. The rest of us defeated our clones, and they also vanished.

Once all the clones were gone, the doors and ceiling opened, and a tree of alabaster grew from the dais. Magical indentations in the floor and walls began to glow white.

Gabriel determined that the pattern in the indentations could be used for containment when we entered.

After it began to glow, it released.

I sensed a powerful soul being released. The Raven Queen pressed into my mind, her presence absolute.

She said: “Your purpose, and mine through you, is to regain my divinity and power.” She expressed curiosity about the soul I had sensed, but added: “Remember this, and do nothing for now.”
