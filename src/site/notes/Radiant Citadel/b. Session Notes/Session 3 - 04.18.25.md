### Journal of [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Seraphina\|Seraphina]]

Kianna was passed out in the cellar, but alive.

The possessed farmers had been knocked out but were revived.

Upstairs, someone handed a haversack to [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Gabriel\|Gabriel]].

I sent my raven statue to deliver a message that we were on our way to Uncle Polder.

---

On the way back, we encountered Uncle Polder and , [[Radiant Citadel/d. NPCs/Sessions 1 - 4/Lady Drae\|Lady Drae]], who was driving her wagon.

We returned to Uncle Polder’s and began resting.

Some in the group noticed a magical disturbance over the town of Promise.

At around 2 AM, we decided to head to Promise with Lady Dre and [[Radiant Citadel/d. NPCs/Sessions 1 - 4/Kianna\|Kianna]].

---

The air was heavy and odd upon arrival.

There was general panic in the area.

Lady Dre and Kianna were sent to the inn while we went to investigate the [[Radiant Citadel/f. Locations/Obelisk\|Obelisk]].

---

The Obelisk had cracks running through it and the ground around it.

A wizard named Alaric, known to Gabriel, was in charge and issuing orders.

He sent colleagues to investigate the Obelisk.

Three died after touching it. Aurelia examined the bodies and found:

- One body: decayed and blackened, showing accelerated necrosis.
    
- Another: peaceful, glowing.
    

 [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Azelph\|Azelph]] and I left to find Lady Dre and Aunt Dellie. The group followed.

---

At the inn, we updated Lady Dre on what had happened at Kianna’s farm and the undead.

The group decided to go to Candlelace Lake to speak with the Proclaimers about:

- The incident and their absence from town
    
- Their claim that they could not have known
    

---

At the Proclaimers Lodge, all of the Proclaimers appeared to be asleep.

They did not believe us at first, but then invited us in.

We told them:

- The Obelisk had exploded
    
- People had died
    

They showed no concern. Instead, they offered snacks and wanted to discuss the Awakening Festival.

---

From the spokesman, we learned:

- The town of Promise is ancient and tied to a god named Jasper. None of us had heard of this god.
    
- The Obelisk is “newer” to the town, not part of the Awakening stories.
    
- They claimed no record of the Obelisk in their lore.
    

---

We requested the oldest Awakening books.

They brought out five volumes.

Gabriel used Detect Magic and discovered a glowing death mask.

The mask contained the memories of an individual and had never been worn.

I offered to wear the mask.

The speaker warned that if I lost my will, he would drown me in the lake.

---

The mask belonged to a mask maker named Essie, who had lived in Promise long ago.

Essie remembered the Obelisk. It was already there, though inert.

She recalled people bringing flowers to its base.

---

The books revealed:

- Ancient gods took the form of animals.
    
- Jasper was a coyote.
    
- The Obelisk was a vessel for Jasper’s power and essence.
    

When confronted with this, the Proclaimer contradicted his earlier claim of no records of the Obelisk.

The group decided to return to town.
