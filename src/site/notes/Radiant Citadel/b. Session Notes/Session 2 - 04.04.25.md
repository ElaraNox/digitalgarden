### Journal of [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Seraphina\|Seraphina]]

I joined the party at Uncle Polder's farmhouse, and together we began travelling to another farmstead in search of [[Radiant Citadel/d. NPCs/Sessions 1 - 4/Kianna\|Kianna]].

As we travelled, the earth tremored beneath us, and a sinkhole opened in the road. [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Aurelia\|Aurelia]] and [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Zamira\|Zamira]] fell in.

The rest of us followed, dropping into the gap, where four [[Radiant Citadel/e. Enemies/Crawling Claw\|Crawling Claw]] attacked. We dispatched them quickly. I collected one for [[Radiant Citadel/d. NPCs/Sessions 1 - 4/Proclaimer Tungsten Ward\|Proclaimer Tungsten Ward]] to study.

---

Our journey continued to the farmhouses. Most stood in disrepair, covered in mud and dead vegetation. All but one had crude red X's painted on them. The singular untouched house was surrounded by green grass.

[[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Gabriel\|Gabriel]] investigated one of the ruined homes. Inside, it was filled with mud and abandoned food. He judged the house had been vacated about a week ago.

Outside, we found human bones scattered across the ground, marked by large animal teeth.

---

We approached the clean house and found its front door unlocked and slightly ajar. Across the floor were muddy handprints forming a rough depiction of two children, one being seized by a large hand and dragged into the lake.

In the kitchen, four farmers stood motionless, their eyes red and unblinking, sharpened farm tools in their hands. They gave no response to speech.

In the dining room, we heard scratching. More Crawling Claws emerged, which we destroyed quickly. We checked the farmers, who still had both of their hands.

In the pantry, we found a trapdoor leading to a root cellar. The southern wall was partially collapsed, and the smell of decay drifted from beyond it. A woman’s voice could be heard singing the awakening song. We pushed through the rubble and followed a tunnel into a chamber.

The bodies of several farmers were heaped in a pile, their limbs removed. In the center stood Kianna, singing, a knife hanging loosely in her hand. She told us to leave.

We tried to draw her away, but the pile of limbs began to move. A grotesque form, the [[Radiant Citadel/e. Enemies/Soul Shaker\|Soul Shaker]], burst free and attacked.

The battle was short. We destroyed the abomination.
