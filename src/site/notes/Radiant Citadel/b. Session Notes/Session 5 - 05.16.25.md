### Journal of [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Seraphina\|Seraphina]]

Proclaimer Thurston came with a picture of a jasper stone on a coast.

At the inn, Alaric reported that nothing had happened near Evereska, where he had contacted a friend.

We decided to head south to a lake nearby.

---
<span style="color:Red"><em>I had a memory of a human male clad in golden armor with a red cloak emblazoned with a gryphon, holding a spear pointed down. I held a greatsword and stood overlooking an army. I shouted, “For Glory.”</em></span>

---

We reached the lake near Promise and set up camp on the shore.

In the morning, we began to search for the stone. On the opposite side, we saw it—roughly the size of a large building. Strange frog-like beings were there, identified as [[Radiant Citadel/e. Enemies/Bullywugs\|Bullywugs]].  [[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Aurelia\|Aurelia]] paid them to allow us access to the stone.

The stone was made of jasper. An archway opened, leading inside. I cast Detect Magic, and the entire interior glowed with every school of magic.

Inside, we found a pillar with magical controls labeled: Land, Embark, Plane Shift.

[[Radiant Citadel/c. Player Characters/Gabriel\|Gabriel]] pressed the Plane Shift button. The gem lifted from the ground and shot into the sky. We then found ourselves outside a city floating in a gray void.

---

We pressed the Land button and descended to a platform jutting into space. Upon landing, a guard approached, speaking in wonder about a Concord Jewel.

He described the city to us, including:

- Speaker Sholeh
    
- Socorro
    
- House of Convalescence
    
- Court of Whispers
    
- Palace of Exile
